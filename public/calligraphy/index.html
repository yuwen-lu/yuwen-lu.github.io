摹帖 · Tracing the masters

Twelve masterpieces, written again stroke by strokeA simulated brush follows the strokes traced from scans of the originals.

Nothing here comes from a font. The tracer reads each scan or rubbing: it separates the ink from the paper and the seals, cuts the columns into characters, thins every character to the centre lines of its strokes and measures how wide each stroke runs. The brush then writes those strokes itself, with bristles, dry streaks and pooled ink, in the original's own layout. See how the tracer works.

Try it

  • Click any character to write it again, one stroke at a time.
  • ←→ a stroke, ⇧ with an arrow a character, Space play or pause.
  • Turn on the 描紅 guide: red left showing is ink the brush missed.
